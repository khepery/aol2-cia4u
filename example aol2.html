🎯 Key Concepts to Research

Thomas Malthus

18th-century economist who believed population growth would outpace food production, leading to inevitable poverty. His pessimistic view suggests scarcity limits economic progress.

18世纪经济学家，认为人口增长会超过食物生产，导致不可避免的贫困。

Learn About Malthus →

Thomas Piketty

Modern economist who argues wealth inequality naturally grows in capitalist economies. He advocates for progressive taxation and wealth redistribution policies.

现代经济学家，认为资本主义经济中财富不平等会自然增长。他主张累进税和财富再分配政策。

Learn About Piketty →

Guaranteed Annual Income

A policy where government provides all citizens with a minimum income regardless of employment. Also known as Universal Basic Income (UBI).

政府向所有公民提供最低收入的政策，无论其就业状况如何。也称为通用基本收入（UBI）。

Learn About UBI →

Microcredit

Small loans provided to low-income individuals to help them start businesses. Pioneered by Muhammad Yunus and the Grameen Bank.

向低收入个人提供的小额贷款，帮助他们创业。由穆罕默德·尤努斯和格莱珉银行开创。

Learn About Microcredit →

Labour Unions

Organizations that represent workers in negotiations with employers. They advocate for better wages, benefits, and working conditions.

代表工人与雇主谈判的组织。他们倡导更好的工资、福利和工作条件。

Learn About Unions →

Tax System Changes

Different approaches to taxation including progressive taxes, flat taxes, wealth taxes, and their effects on inequality and economic growth.

不同的税收方法，包括累进税、固定税、财富税，以及它们对不平等和经济增长的影响。

Learn About Taxation →
